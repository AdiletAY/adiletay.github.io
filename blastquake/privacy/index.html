BlastQuake Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

BlastQuake (“the game”) is a puzzle game for mobile phones, made by thejustise (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the game handles information. If you have any questions, write to thejustise04@gmail.com.

In short: the game has no accounts, and we don’t collect your personal information or run any servers. Your progress is saved only on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to show them, as described below.

1. Information saved on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves one file in its private storage on your device. This is everything it contains:

  • Best score: the highest score you have reached.
  • All-time statistics, added up over your finished games: total pieces placed, total lines cleared, total merges, the biggest tile you have made, and your longest cascade (the most merges from a single move).
  • Coins: how many coins you have, the date you last received the daily coin reward, and how many days in a row you have received it.
  • Banner timing: how many games you have finished since the last banner ad, so the game knows when to show the next one.
  • The game in progress, if you left one unfinished: its score, the number on every tile on the board, the pieces left to place and the numbers on them, its move order, whether its one revive has been used, and its statistics so far (pieces placed, lines cleared, merges, biggest tile, longest cascade, and how many moves earned a combo).
  • Settings: the language you picked in the game (nothing is saved until you pick one; until then the game follows your phone’s language), whether sound is on, and the move order (whether full lines clear before tiles merge, or after).

The file holds nothing else: no name, email address, account, contacts, location or device identifier. Your answer to the ad consent form (section 3), if you are asked, is also kept on your device by Google’s consent tool, so you aren’t asked again on every launch.

This file is never sent to us. If you have turned on backup on your Android device, Android may include it in your device backup to your Google account, under Google’s terms. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your device settings, deletes it.

2. Advertising: Google AdMob

The game shows banner ads and, if you choose to watch one, rewarded ads (for example, to continue a game). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK in the game may collect and share with Google:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • device information, such as model, operating system version and language;
  • your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic information about the ads SDK, such as crashes and performance.

This information is sent encrypted. Google uses it as described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. We only see combined reports from AdMob, such as how many ads were shown, which don’t identify you.

3. Your choices

  • Ad consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before ads may use your information for personalized advertising. You can change your choice later from the game’s settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s settings (on most devices, Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads). Without it, ads are not personalized.
  • Deleting your data: uninstall the game, or clear its storage in your device settings. We hold no copy, so there is nothing else to delete on our side.

4. Permissions

The game uses internet and network-state access only to load ads. It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.

5. Sharing

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t share it with anyone. The only other party that receives information from the game is Google, as our ad provider (section 2), and as the operator of Google Play, under its own terms.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Security and retention

Your saved progress stays in the game’s private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. We don’t keep any of your information. Google keeps the information it collects for ads according to its own policy.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
